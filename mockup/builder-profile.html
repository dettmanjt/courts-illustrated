How rankings work

Each builder gets a Courts Illustrated score out of 100. It rewards experience you can check, plus the time builders spend teaching buyers. Company scores are the average of their builders.

10 points each, up to two. For example, ASBA Certified Tennis Court Builder or ASBA membership.
