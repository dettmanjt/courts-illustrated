My wife Kristine and I own and run Court Builders of the Rockies. We design and build residential and commercial courts, gym floors and turf across Colorado, Wyoming and the Rocky Mountain region. We build on engineered post-tension concrete, and sports construction is all we do.

In 2025, Racquet Sports Industry named me Builder/Contractor of the Year in its Champions of Tennis awards. My advice to other builders: "If your people are good, your product will flourish. Give employees the education they need to do the kind of job you want."
