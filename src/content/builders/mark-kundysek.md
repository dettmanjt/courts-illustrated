I was working for a plastics company in Grand Prairie in 1988 when I first saw a sample of modular tile surfacing for athletic courts. That curiosity led me to buy a $250 dealer kit and launch NexCourt, now one of the pioneers of sports construction in North Texas and Oklahoma.

I'm a graduate of Texas Tech University and live in Arlington, TX with my wife Kristy and our two sons.
